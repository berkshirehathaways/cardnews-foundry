# Editorial method

## Build the brief

- State one audience and one defensible thesis.
- Link every factual claim to one or more valid sanitized source-span IDs.
- Separate sourced fact from interpretation.
- Put unsupported, unverifiable, or out-of-scope claims in exclusions.
- Fit the target profile’s safe card-count range.
- Preserve meaning across Korean line breaks; do not optimize wording for layout until the claim remains accurate.

## Build the storyboard

- Give the sequence a clear hook, progression, and closing action or synthesis.
- Assign one primary communication job per card.
- Inherit claims and source-span links from the committed brief.
- Remove repetition rather than disguising it with different visuals.
- Describe semantic scenes and hierarchy only. Do not add HTML, CSS, coordinates, pixels, image-provider prompts, or renderer internals.
- Keep quotations short and exact. Prefer paraphrase with attribution where quotation is unnecessary.

## Revise copy

Create a new downstream revision. Preserve source and brief checkpoints when their meaning and evidence links remain valid. Recommit every changed dependent record; never mutate an immutable receipt.
